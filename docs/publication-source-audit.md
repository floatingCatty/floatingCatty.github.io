## Important version details

- The official ICLR 2025 SLEM proceedings index lists five authors under
  the first-author spelling **YinZhangHao Zhou**. The earlier
  [arXiv:2407.06053](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.06053) version lists six,
  including MingKang Liu. Both are linked in the publication record,
  but the conference citation follows its official proceedings.
- The NQS preprint initially used another first-author transliteration;
  the published APS article uses **Zhanghao Zhouyin**. Do not collapse or
  rewrite a paper's formal byline merely to match the website name.
- The DeePTB GaP example has **one million (10^6) atoms** in the
  learned-tight-binding property calculation, not 106 atoms and not
  a million-atom self-consistent DFT calculation.
- VBCSR benchmark results depend on block structure, matrix shapes,
  hardware and chosen competitors. Do not present one reported speedup as
  a general performance guarantee.
- The graphene/h-BN/MoS2 DeePTB-NEGF paper was first available on
  arXiv in December 2025 and is also a **published IEEE MoSICom 2025
  conference paper**, DOI 10.1109/MoSICom67153.2025.11398312 (added to
  IEEE Xplore 2 March 2026). The old stable publication-page permalink
  intentionally remains unchanged to avoid breaking links.
- DeePTB-NEGF reports results for graphene, h-BN and MoS2. Do not
  generalize the stated accuracy to arbitrary junctions, defects or
  industrial device designs without separate validation.

## Site summaries versus original abstracts

Several older `_publications/` entries contain author-written site
summaries or earlier manuscript-era descriptions rather than a
word-for-word copy of the final publisher abstract. The newly added VBCSR preprint and the 2D transport conference paper
record are **original site summaries**, not reproductions of the source
abstracts.

Do not label these summaries `Publisher abstract` or claim they are exact
reproductions of the published abstracts. Google Scholar's inclusion
requirements are more specific: it generally needs the complete,
author-written abstract or the full paper to be freely visible on a
separate URL. A bibliography summary plus citation metadata does not, by
itself, guarantee inclusion. To bring these pages into full compliance,
the corresponding authors should confirm an abstract text they have the
right to host, or provide an eligible manuscript PDF. Do not copy
publisher text without reviewing its license and applicable author rights.

The website should keep its brief **research explanations** separate from
publication records. Adding new public papers should include a source
URL, correct author list, unambiguous published versus preprint status
and a linked research note if one exists.
