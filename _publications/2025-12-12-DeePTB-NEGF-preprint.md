Quantum transport calculations often require an electronic Hamiltonian as input, and constructing it from self-consistent first-principles calculations can be expensive. This preprint combines a DeePTB-learned tight-binding Hamiltonian with the DPNEGF transport solver. It reports comparisons of band structures and electronic transmission against conventional DFT-NEGF calculations for graphene, hexagonal boron nitride and MoS2, together with lower computational costs in the examples studied. The results are from the public preprint; this page does not imply journal acceptance.
