Quantum transport calculations often require an electronic Hamiltonian as input, and constructing it from self-consistent first-principles calculations can be expensive. This conference paper combines a DeePTB-learned tight-binding Hamiltonian with the DPNEGF transport solver. It compares band structures and electronic transmission with DFT-NEGF calculations for graphene, hexagonal boron nitride and MoS2, reporting lower computational cost for these test systems. The work was presented at MoSICom 2025 and entered IEEE Xplore on 2 March 2026. The arXiv version remains available; this page does not claim a separate journal publication.
