I'm **Zhanghao Zhouyin (周寅张皓)**, a Physics PhD candidate at McGill University. I began in computer science, moved into electronic-structure and quantum-transport research, and gradually found myself spending as much time on numerical solvers and scientific software as on machine learning.

I want to make it easier to go from a physical model to something a researcher can actually measure or compare with experiment. That means working across several layers: predicting quantum operators, computing electronic properties from them, and making the algorithms run on materials large enough to show interesting behavior.

Some papers use the author name **Yinzhanghao Zhou**. Both English names refer to me.

I keep brief notes about these projects in the [research section]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}). They include [DeePTB]({{ '/research/deeptb/' | relative_url }}), [SLEM]({{ '/research/slem/' | relative_url }}), [AD-NEGF]({{ '/research/ad-negf/' | relative_url }}), work on [moiré phonons]({{ '/research/moire-phonon/' | relative_url }}) and [quantum embedding]({{ '/research/nqs-impurity/' | relative_url }}), and the software projects [VBCSR]({{ '/research/vbcsr/' | relative_url }}) and [flower]({{ '/research/flower/' | relative_url }}). For the papers themselves, see [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) or [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=vSMmfvUAAAAJ). The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/floatingCatty).
