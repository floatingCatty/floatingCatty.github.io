AI 原生科学计算与研究项目AI-native scientific computing and research projects

  • DeePTB + SLEM · 2022–2025. 共同开发面向百万原子模拟的学习型紧束缚模型（Nature Communications 2024，共同第一作者），主导用于量子算符的局域等变模型 SLEM（ICLR 2025 Spotlight，第一作者）。Co-developed learned tight-binding electronic simulation for million-atom systems (Nature Communications 2024, co-first author) and led the localized equivariant SLEM quantum-operator model (ICLR 2025 Spotlight, first author).
  • VBCSR · 2026. 设计面向原子轨道的 MPI 分布式变块稀疏矩阵引擎；在测试的稀疏矩阵乘法算例中最高达到 8.4 倍加速，并用于 1,063,609 原子 InP 纳米颗粒的电子态计算。Designed an MPI-distributed variable-block sparse-matrix engine for orbital calculations, reporting up to 8.4× speedup in the tested sparse-multiplication benchmarks and an application to a 1,063,609-atom InP nanoparticle.
  • RESCU++ · 2026–present. 开发基于 C++/Python 与 VBCSR 的 LCAO-DFT 平台，覆盖分子、周期材料、磁性、自旋轨道耦合与杂化泛函；探索 GP 自洽加速、贝叶斯结构优化和自动化测试。Developing a C++/Python LCAO-DFT platform built on VBCSR for molecular and periodic systems, magnetism, spin–orbit coupling and hybrid functionals, with Gaussian-process SCF acceleration, Bayesian relaxation and automated validation.
  • RSATB · 2026–present. 研究无对角化的非正交哈密顿量谱、空间分辨物性及线性响应计算；正在研究超大尺度扭转三层石墨烯体系，论文准备中。Developing diagonalization-free spectra, spatial electronic maps and response properties from large nonorthogonal Hamiltonians; large-scale twisted-trilayer-graphene study in progress, manuscript in preparation.
  • AD-NEGF. 端到端可微的非平衡格林函数量子输运模拟器（Physical Review B 2023）。End-to-end differentiable nonequilibrium Green's-function quantum-transport simulation (Physical Review B 2023).
  • flower. 记录 Agent 在本地、SSH 和 Slurm 上完成的计算，并导出可复现工作流。Records scientific-agent calculations across local, SSH and Slurm systems and exports auditable, rerunnable protocols.
