AI-native scientific computing and research projects

  • DeePTB + SLEM · 2022–2025. Co-developed learned tight-binding electronic simulation for million-atom systems (Nature Communications 2024, co-first author) and led the localized equivariant SLEM quantum-operator model (ICLR 2025 Spotlight, first author).
  • VBCSR · 2026. Designed an MPI-distributed variable-block sparse-matrix engine for orbital calculations, reporting up to 8.4× speedup in the tested sparse-multiplication benchmarks and an application to a 1,063,609-atom InP nanoparticle.
  • RESCU++ · 2026–present. Developing a C++/Python LCAO-DFT platform built on VBCSR for molecular and periodic systems, magnetism, spin–orbit coupling and hybrid functionals, with Gaussian-process SCF acceleration, Bayesian relaxation and automated validation.
  • RSATB · 2026–present. Developing diagonalization-free spectra, spatial electronic maps and response properties from large nonorthogonal Hamiltonians; large-scale twisted-trilayer-graphene study in progress, manuscript in preparation.
  • AD-NEGF. End-to-end differentiable nonequilibrium Green's-function quantum-transport simulation (Physical Review B 2023).
  • flower. Records scientific-agent calculations across local, SSH and Slurm systems and exports auditable, rerunnable protocols.
