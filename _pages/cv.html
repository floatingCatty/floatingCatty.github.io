研究方向Research areas

  • AI 增强的电子结构Machine-learned electronic structure. 用局域模型预测哈密顿量和量子算符（DeePTB、SLEM），并研究更大体系的计算方法。Predicting Hamiltonians and quantum operators from atomic environments, including DeePTB and SLEM.
  • 大规模数值算法Scalable numerical methods. 分布式稀疏矩阵、线性代数、电子态与物性计算（VBCSR 等）。Distributed sparse matrices, numerical linear algebra, and electronic-property calculations (including VBCSR).
  • 量子输运与可微模拟Quantum transport and differentiable simulation. NEGF、AD-NEGF，以及从学习到的哈密顿量到输运物理量的计算。NEGF, AD-NEGF, and computing transport observables from learned Hamiltonians.
  • 可复现的科学计算Reproducible scientific computing. 让研究人员与 AI Agent 在本地和计算集群上运行、记录、复现计算（flower）。Tools for researchers and AI agents to record, run and reproduce calculations across local and remote systems (flower).
