几项研究的来龙去脉The stories behind a few research projects

这里是我参与的几项工作。我暂时把论文里的公式放在一边，先说清楚我们究竟想算什么、为什么难算，以及论文里实际做到了哪一步。算法和测试结果都可以从文末的论文链接找到。Here are a few projects I worked on. I have left the equations aside for a moment to explain what we wanted to calculate, why it was difficult, and what we actually managed to do. The papers linked on each page have the algorithms and tests.

2024 · Nature Communications

一百万个原子的电子结构，怎么算？How do you calculate electronic structure in a million-atom material?

高温下原子不断振动。DeePTB 从较小体系的第一性原理能级学习紧束缚参数，用它计算更大材料里的电子性质。Atoms vibrate as temperature rises. DeePTB learns tight-binding parameters from first-principles energy levels of smaller systems, then uses them to calculate electronic properties in larger materials.

原子轨道有方向，AI 怎么学电子结构？Atomic orbitals have directions. How can AI learn electronic structure?

晶体转个方向，轨道矩阵也要按物理规律变换。SLEM 把局域性和旋转对称性放进模型，用来预测量子算符矩阵。When a crystal rotates, its orbital matrices must follow the physical transformation rules. SLEM builds locality and rotational symmetry into the prediction of quantum-operator matrices.

读这篇Read the story ↗ 2023 · Physical Review B

算出了电流，下一步怎么改器件？We've calculated the current. What should we change in the device?

NEGF 能从器件模型计算电流。AD-NEGF 给求解器增加梯度，用来分析哪些模型参数影响输运，以及如何反过来寻找目标哈密顿量。NEGF calculates current from a device model. AD-NEGF adds gradients to the solver to examine which model parameters affect transport and how to search for a target Hamiltonian.
