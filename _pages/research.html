我的研究，讲清楚一点The questions behind the papers

论文往往从公式与术语开始，我更想从问题开始。下面这几篇短文谈谈为什么做这些工作，它们解决了什么，以及还有哪些事情没有解决。Papers often begin with equations and terminology. Here I start with the questions: why we worked on these problems, what we found, and what remains difficult.
